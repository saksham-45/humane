One of these was written by a person.

HUMANE is a daily pair. Two columns, same topic. One side is a human text with a named source. The other is machine prose written offline. You pick the human. A correct cut keeps a streak. A miss wipes it.

There is one pair per UTC day, shared by everyone. Not a chat. Not a quiz of ten. The label never leaves the server until your guess is in. After that you get the tell, the source, and the model.

The human side is public-domain or clearly licensed: Gutenberg, pre-1929 essays, old speeches, Wikimedia-licensed short nonfiction. No invented “human.” No live generation. The paper changes at midnight UTC.
